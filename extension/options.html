    How to use

    Slack workspaces

    Choose Desktop to close the redirect tab after Slack opens, or Browser to keep that workspace in the browser. New workspaces appear after you open a Slack link, or you can add one above. Choices are saved separately in each browser profile.

    Zoom

    Join tabs close when Zoom Workplace opens. Choose “Join from browser” before the short delay ends to keep the meeting in the browser.

    Install or update

    1. Open your browser’s extensions page (usually chrome://extensions).
    2. Enable Developer mode and choose Load unpacked.
    3. Select this extension’s extension folder. After an update, reload its card.
